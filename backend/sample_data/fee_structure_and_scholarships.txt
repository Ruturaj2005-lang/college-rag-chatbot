## 4. Examination & Administrative Fees
- Semester Examination Fee: INR 1,500 per semester, payable prior to admit card release.
- Late Examination Fee: INR 500 late surcharge after the announced deadline.
- Re-evaluation / Re-totalling Fee: INR 750 per subject course.
- Laboratory & Library Maintenance Fee: INR 3,000 per year.
- Training & Placement Registration Fee: INR 2,000 (one-time in final year).

## 5. Payment Schedule & Deadlines
- Autumn/Odd Semester Fee Deadline: August 15 each academic year.
- Spring/Even Semester Fee Deadline: January 15 each academic year.
- A grace period of 7 days is permitted, after which a late fee of INR 100 per day is applied.

## 6. Merit & Need-Based Scholarships
- **President's Merit Scholarship**: 100% tuition fee waiver for students ranking in top 500 in state/national entrance exams or scoring 95%+ in Class 12.
- **Dean's Honors Scholarship**: 50% tuition waiver for semester SGPA >= 9.2 with no active backlogs across B.Tech, B.Sc, M.Tech, and M.Sc.
- **GATE Fellowship for M.Tech**: Monthly AICTE stipend of INR 12,400 for all GATE-qualified M.Tech candidates.
- **Women in STEM Excellence Award**: 25% tuition fee waiver for top 20 female students in STEM programs.
- **Financial Need Grant**: Up to 40% tuition fee concession for families with annual gross income below INR 3.5 Lakhs.
- **Sports Scholarship**: 30% fee concession for national and state-level sports medal winners.
Renewal of all scholarships requires maintaining a minimum CGPA of 7.5 and 75% class attendance.
