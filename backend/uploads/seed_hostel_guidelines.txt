# College Hostel Guidelines and Residential Rules

## 1. Hostel Accommodation Overview
The College provides residential accommodation for undergraduate and postgraduate students. Hostel allotment is carried out on a merit-cum-distance basis during admission.

## 2. Hostel Fees & Security Deposit
- Annual Hostel Rent & Amenities: INR 50,000 per academic year (INR 25,000 payable per semester).
- Refundable Caution Deposit: INR 5,000 (one-time upon admission).
- Mess Charges: INR 3,500 per month, billed on a semester advance basis.
- Mode of Payment: Online through the college ERP student portal before the semester start date.

## 3. Mess Schedule & Timings
- Breakfast: 07:30 AM to 09:00 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM to 02:00 PM
- Evening Snacks & Tea: 04:30 PM to 05:30 PM
- Dinner: 07:30 PM to 09:30 PM
Special diet is available on medical grounds with prior written intimation to the Chief Warden.

## 4. Hostel Curfew & Gate In-Timings
- The main hostel gates will be locked strictly at 09:30 PM for Boys Hostels and 09:00 PM for Girls Hostels.
- Late entry requires prior written permission from the Chief Warden.
- Night-outs and weekend home leaves must be logged through the Digital Leave Application portal 24 hours in advance with parent/guardian confirmation.

## 5. Discipline & Prohibited Items
- Possession or consumption of alcohol, narcotics, tobacco, or e-cigarettes is strictly prohibited on campus premises and will lead to immediate expulsion.
- Heavy electrical appliances such as heaters, induction stoves, and immersion rods are not allowed in rooms without written electrical approval.
- Quiet hours must be observed between 10:30 PM and 06:00 AM in all corridors and study halls.
- Visitors and day-scholars are strictly prohibited from entering student hostel rooms without warden clearance.
