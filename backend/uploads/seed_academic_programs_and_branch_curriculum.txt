# Academic Programs, Degrees and Branch Curriculum Catalog

## 1. Undergraduate B.Tech Programs (4 Years / 8 Semesters)
- **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Data Structures & Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming with Java/C++, Operating Systems, Computer Architecture, Database Management Systems (DBMS), Formal Language & Automata, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Compiler Design.
  - *Advanced Electives*: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Blockchain, Natural Language Processing.
  - *Credits*: Total 160 Credits across 8 semesters. Includes 3 Mini-Projects and an 8-credit Capstone Major Project.

- **B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Python for Data Science, Linear Algebra & Probability, Foundations of AI, Machine Learning Algorithms, Deep Neural Networks, Computer Vision, Big Data Analytics with Hadoop & Spark, Reinforcement Learning.
  - *Labs*: AI Lab, GPU Computing Lab, Data Engineering & Pipeline Lab.

- **B.Tech in Information Technology (IT)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Full-Stack Web Technologies (React, Node.js), Distributed Cloud Architecture, Information & Network Security, Mobile Computing (Flutter/Android), DevOps and Agile Practices, Enterprise Systems.

- **B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Digital Electronics, Analog Circuit Design, Signals & Systems, Microprocessors (8086/ARM), Digital Signal Processing (DSP), VLSI Design, Wireless & Cellular Communications, Microwave Engineering, Optical Fiber Communications.

- **B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Electric Circuit Analysis, Power Generation & Transmission, Electrical Machines (DC/AC), Control Systems Engineering, Power Electronics, Smart Grid Technology, Renewable Energy Systems, Electric Vehicle (EV) Engineering.

- **B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (ME)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Engineering Thermodynamics, Strength of Materials, Kinematics & Dynamics of Machines, Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics, Heat and Mass Transfer, CAD/CAM/CAE Modeling, Manufacturing Technology, Robotics & Automation.

- **B.Tech in Civil Engineering (CE)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Structural Analysis, Concrete Technology & Design of Steel Structures, Geotechnical & Soil Engineering, Environmental Engineering & Water Resource Management, Transportation Engineering, Surveying and Remote Sensing / GIS.

- **B.Tech in Biotechnology (BT)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Cell Biology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Genetic Engineering & Recombinant DNA, Bioinformatics, Bioprocess Engineering & Fermentation Technology, Immunology, Plant and Animal Tissue Culture.

## 2. Undergraduate B.Sc and BCA Programs (3 Years / 6 Semesters)
- **B.Sc in Computer Science (Hons)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Programming Fundamentals in C & C++, Discrete Mathematics, Data Structures, Computer Organization, Database Concepts, Web Development Basics, Linux Shell Scripting, Computer Networks, Python Programming.
  - *Total Credits*: 120 Credits. Includes Project Work in Semester 6.

- **B.Sc in Data Science & Analytics**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Applied Statistics, Python and R Programming, Exploratory Data Analysis, SQL & NoSQL Databases, Introduction to Machine Learning, Data Visualization using Tableau & PowerBI, Business Analytics.

- **B.Sc in Information Technology (IT)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: IT Infrastructure, Network Fundamentals, Open Source Technologies, Cyber Laws & Ethics, Database Administration, Cloud Fundamentals.

- **B.Sc in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Classical Mechanics, Quantum Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Optics, Organic & Inorganic Chemistry, Physical Chemistry, Calculus, Differential Equations, Linear Algebra, Real Analysis.

- **B.Sc in Biotechnology & Microbiology**:
  - *Core Subjects*: General Microbiology, Molecular Genetics, Microbial Physiology, Bioinstrumentation, Industrial Microbiology, Enzymology, Environmental Biotechnology.

- **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)**:
  - *Core Subjects*: Programming in C/C++/Java, Web Technology, Relational Databases (Oracle/MySQL), Software Engineering Principles, Computer Graphics, Mobile Application Development.

## 5. Grading System, CGPA Calculation & Academic Evaluation
- Grading Scale: 10-Point Scale (O: 10.0 - Outstanding, A+: 9.0 - Excellent, A: 8.0 - Very Good, B+: 7.0 - Good, B: 6.0 - Above Average, C: 5.0 - Average/Pass, F: 0.0 - Fail).
- SGPA Formula: SGPA = Sum(Course Credit * Grade Point) / Total Semester Credits.
- CGPA Formula: Cumulative Grade Point Average calculated across all completed semesters.
- Passing Standard: Minimum 40% in Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and 40% in Semester End Examination (SEE) with an aggregate of 50%.
