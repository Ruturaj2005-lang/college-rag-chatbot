# College Tuition Fee Structure and Scholarship Policies

## 1. Academic Tuition Fees (Annual)
- B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): INR 1,20,000 per year (INR 60,000 per semester).
- B.Tech in Information Technology & AI: INR 1,15,000 per year (INR 57,500 per semester).
- B.Tech in Electronics & Mechanical Engineering: INR 1,00,000 per year (INR 50,000 per semester).
- Master of Computer Applications (MCA): INR 85,000 per year.
- Master of Technology (M.Tech): INR 90,000 per year.
- B.Sc in Agriculture: INR 90,000 per year

## 2. Examination & Administrative Fees
- Examination Fee: INR 1,500 per semester, payable prior to admit card download.
- Late Examination Fee: INR 500 late surcharge after the announced deadline.
- Re-evaluation / Re-totalling Fee: INR 750 per subject course.
- Library & Lab Development Fee: INR 3,000 per year.

## 3. Payment Schedule & Deadlines
- Autumn/Odd Semester Fee Deadline: August 15 each academic year.
- Spring/Even Semester Fee Deadline: January 15 each academic year.
- A grace period of 7 days is permitted, after which a late fee of INR 100 per day is applied.

## 4. Merit & Need-Based Scholarships
- **President's Merit Scholarship**: 100% tuition fee waiver for students ranking in top 500 in state engineering entrance or scoring 95%+ in Class 12 PCM.
- **Dean's Honors Scholarship**: 50% tuition waiver for semester SGPA >= 9.2 with no active backlogs.
- **Women in STEM Excellence Award**: 25% tuition fee waiver for top 20 female engineering students.
- **Financial Need Grant**: Up to 40% tuition assistance for families with annual gross income below INR 3.5 Lakhs.
- **Sports Scholarship**: 30% fee concession for national and state-level sports medal winners.
All scholarship renewal requires maintaining a minimum CGPA of 7.5 and 75% class attendance throughout the academic term.
